# Observation Director And Playback Hardening

## Objective

Strengthen the committed time foundation and reserve observable camera/work/dwell phases while preserving historical anchors.

## Scope

- Replace the discontinuous fast-forward progress curve with an integrated continuous rate envelope bounded by the declared peak rate.
- Expose presentation progress independently of frozen historical progress, stable timestamp-group IDs, strict event identities and copied payloads.
- Budget preparation, camera travel, presentation work and dwell; same-time events are available together and observed separately.
- Provide a guarded normal-play step that stops at visit boundaries and discards excess suspended-tab wall time instead of skipping observations.

## Dependencies

- [Bounded implementation authorization](../001-4-bounded-host-neutral-foundation-implementation-authorization-decision.trace.md).
- [Runtime Productization master](../001-playthings-runtime-productization-task.trace.md).
- The exact uploaded Playthings source; frozen dependency workspaces are context only.

## Done Criteria

- Executable fixtures cover the scope and explicit failure modes without external dependencies.
- Inputs remain internal, replaceable Playthings fixture/adapter shapes.
- Source and findings are preserved in the child Evidence, with original source paths retained.

## Exclusions

- No browser input binding, renderer frame acknowledgement, hot-pace-switch UI, live-import policy or fullscreen implementation.
- Low-level time sampling remains useful for explicit seek; only the observation guard is the normal-play no-skip surface.
